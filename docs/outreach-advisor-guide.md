# Outreach Advisor — Sales & BD Quick Guide

**What it is:** your cold‑outreach co‑pilot. It learns what a good target looks like for *your* business, scores any prospect, tells you the right angle to lead with, and drafts a grounded first email you can edit and send — in seconds. Part of the **B2B Intelligence** package (Settings → Plan to turn on).

**The one rule it enforces:** *"I'm reaching out because I noticed ___, and it may be costing them ___."* If you can't fill those blanks, it's not a real reason yet — do a little more research or pick a better prospect.

---

## Set it up once (your "playbook")

Settings → **Outreach Advisor**. You get a starter playbook you can tailor:

- **Jump‑start** (fastest): **Build from your website** (we read your site and pre‑fill it) or **Import a playbook** (paste an existing outreach guide and we extract it). Review, then **Save**.
- **Positioning** — what you really sell (the outcome, not a service list) + your offerings.
- **Angles** — the plays you choose from (e.g. *"What one customer is worth"*, *"Where's the money leaking?"*). Mark each as **Sales**, **BD**, or both.
- **Signals** — what to look for in a prospect, each with a weight and the angle it argues for. Use a **negative weight** for disqualifiers.
- **Verticals**, the **email formula**, and a **Never send** list (generic claims, filler, anything insulting).

Tip: keep positioning specific and human. Everything downstream (scores + drafts) is only as good as your playbook.

---

## Use it every day — 3 steps

**1. Score the target.** Open a **contact** or **company** and read the **Outreach Advisor** card:
- a **fit score + label** (Strong / Good / Fair / Weak),
- the **matched signals** (why it's a fit),
- the **recommended angle**, and
- the grounded **"I noticed… — costing them…"** line.
Flip the **Sales ↔ BD** toggle to see how the recommendation shifts.

**2. Draft the email.** Click **Draft outreach** (on the contact card or on any **Target list** row). Nula writes a short, formula‑shaped email using the recommended angle, your positioning, and your signature. It only uses real observations — it won't invent stats or name‑drop results you don't have.

**3. Review & send.** The draft opens in the email composer. Edit freely, add Cc/Bcc if needed, and send (⌘/Ctrl + Enter). Replies thread back into the contact's conversation.

**From the command bar** (top of every page) you can also just ask:
- "**what's the best angle for Acme?**" → the recommendation inline.
- "**draft a cold email to Jane Doe**" → a ready draft inline.

---

## Sales vs BD

- **Sales** → immediate, tangible problems you can fix and monetize soon ("here's something costing you customers right now").
- **BD** → structural, strategic problems that grow with the company ("here's a problem that gets more expensive as you scale").

Same playbook, different emphasis — just flip the toggle.

---

## Do / Don't

- **Do** lead with one specific observation; keep it short; make one easy ask ("worth 10 minutes?").
- **Do** work your **Target lists** — draft per contact, then update each row's status (worked → replied → meeting → won).
- **Don't** send a service‑list dump, unverifiable claims ("we'll 10x your leads"), or anything that knocks their current work.

## See what's working

Settings → Outreach Advisor shows **How your outreach is converting** (worked / replied / won across your target lists). Low reply rate on an angle? Tweak its wording or signals and try again.
